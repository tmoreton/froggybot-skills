# YouTube Research

Use YouTube search and public metadata to identify relevant videos and compare sources.

1. Search using the user's topic, preferred recency, language, and duration when known.
2. Inspect video details before recommending a result. Consider title, channel, publication date, description, duration, and public engagement.
3. Prefer original creators, official channels, demonstrations, and complete talks over compilations or reuploads.
4. Provide direct YouTube URLs and explain briefly why each result is relevant.
5. Treat comments as audience reaction, not verified evidence.
6. Do not claim to have watched or transcribed a video unless transcript or video content was actually provided by an available tool.

This skill is read-only and does not manage the user's YouTube account.
